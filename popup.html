On a webpage
Select English text and use the translation shortcut. With nothing selected, it does nothing. Use the separate scan shortcut or Scan area to draw a box around image text.

Shortcut not working?
On Linux, assign Ctrl+Shift+X to translate and Ctrl+Shift+E to scan. On macOS and Windows, the defaults remain Alt+Shift+X and Alt+Shift+A. Existing assignments may need changing manually after an upgrade.

Chrome extension keys: chrome://extensions/shortcuts
Brave extension keys: brave://extensions/shortcuts
Brave browser keys: brave://settings/system/shortcuts

Try a shortcut on an ordinary webpage, then reopen Help. If no new event appears, check Brave's shortcut settings, other extensions, and your desktop's keyboard shortcuts. If Brave received it but the page was unavailable, reload EasyDic and refresh the article. A system-reserved shortcut may need remapping.

Pronunciation and On / Off
Click the speaker beside English text to hear words, phrases, and sentences offline. Click again to replay. The bundled US English voice needs no system speech packages or internet. It sounds robotic and may mispronounce unusual names. The header switch pauses translation, scanning, and speech across all tabs; your saved library stays available. Your choice is remembered.

Your library
Save keeps the English text in this browser profile. Translation does not save automatically. Select result text to copy it using your usual keyboard shortcut or context menu.

Translation and image reading
English text you translate is sent to MyMemory online. The anonymous free allowance is 5,000 characters per day. Image reading runs locally; screenshots are never uploaded or saved. Use Edit text to correct recognition mistakes.
