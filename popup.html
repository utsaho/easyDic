On a webpage
Select English text and use the translation shortcut. With nothing selected, it does nothing. Use the separate scan shortcut or Scan area to draw a box around image text.

Shortcut not working?
In your browser's extension shortcut settings, assign Alt+Shift+X to “Translate selected text” and Alt+Shift+A to “Scan image area”, or choose other available keys.

Chrome: chrome://extensions/shortcuts
Brave: brave://extensions/shortcuts

Refresh the article after reloading EasyDic. Use an ordinary webpage, check competing extensions, and check your system's keyboard shortcuts. A system-reserved shortcut may need to be remapped.

Your library
Save keeps the English text in this browser profile. Translation does not save automatically. Select result text to copy it using your usual keyboard shortcut or context menu.

Translation and image reading
English text you translate is sent to MyMemory online. The anonymous free allowance is 5,000 characters per day. Image reading runs locally; screenshots are never uploaded or saved. Use Edit text to correct recognition mistakes.
